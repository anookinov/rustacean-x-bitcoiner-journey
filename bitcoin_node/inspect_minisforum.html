Inspecting Minisforum Hardware

This guide provides clear, step-by-step instructions to inspect your Minisforum Mini PC hardware before installation or use. Careful inspection ensures all components are present, undamaged, and ready for your Bitcoin node or server setup.

In this guide, you will:

  • Prepare a static-free workspace
  • Open the Mini PC case and identify key components
  • Verify RAM, SSD, and OcuLink hardware
  • Document hardware details for future reference

Pre-requisites

Preparation

  • Set up a clean, static-free workspace
  • Have a small Phillips screwdriver available

Hardware

  • Minisforum AI X1-255 Mini PC (with pre-installed 1TB NVMe SSD)
  • OcuLink chip/port (included in the Minisforum package)
  • Camera or smartphone for documenting hardware details (optional)

Software

  • No software required

References

  • Minisforum X1-255 Product Page
  • Minisforum Support & Manuals

Step‑by‑Step Instructions

Step 1: Unbox and Check Package Contents

  1. Open the Minisforum AI X1-255 package.
  1. Carefully remove all items and lay them out on your static-free workspace.

🧤 Static-Free Workspace: Work in a static-free environment to avoid damaging sensitive components.

  1. Verify that the following items are included:

    • Minisforum AI X1-255 Mini PC (with pre-installed 1TB NVMe SSD)
    • Power supply unit (PSU) and power cable
    • OcuLink chip/port module
    • HDMI cable (included)
    • Accessories (e.g., screws, mounting brackets, documentation)

📸 Take Photos: Take photos of the internal components for your records if needed.

  1. Check that all items are undamaged and present.
  1. Locate the four screws on the back panel of Minisforum AI X1-255 Mini PC case.

⚡️ Warranty Notice: Opening the case may void the manufacturer’s warranty. Check warranty terms before proceeding if this is a concern.

  1. Gently unscrew and remove the back panel to access the internal components.

🔩 Screw Storage Tip: Keep the screws in a safe place while the case is open to avoid losing them.

Step 2: Check Pre-installed RAM

  1. Verify the amount and type of RAM currently installed in the SODIMM slots.
  2. The Mini PC may have one or two RAM modules pre-installed.
  3. Note the specifications (e.g., 16GB DDR5, DDR4, etc.) for your records.

🧠 RAM Upgrade Tip: The Mini PC has two SODIMM slots. If you need more RAM in the future, you can upgrade to 64GB or higher depending on motherboard support.

Step 3: Check Pre-installed NVMe Storage

  1. The MINISFORUM X1-255 comes with a 1TB NVMe SSD pre-installed in one of the two M.2 2280 NVMe slots.

💾 NVMe Storage Tip: Both M.2 2280 NVMe slots can be used for SSDs or for installing the OcuLink chip. If you need more storage, you can replace the existing drive with a larger capacity SSD (2TB, 4TB, etc.), or add a second SSD for expansion or RAID-1 mirroring.

  1. Note the capacity and model of the SSD for your records.
  2. Ensure the SSD is firmly seated in its slot.
  3. Locate the second M.2 2280 NVMe slot. As only one can be installed in the second slot at a time, decide whether you want to:
  • Add a second SSD (for more storage or RAID-1)
  • Or install the OcuLink chip (for high-speed external storage)

🛠️ Expansion Notice: If you plan for internal mirroring (RAID-1), plan to install a second, matching-capacity NVMe SSD in this slot later. If you want to use OcuLink, proceed to the next step.

Step 4: Install OcuLink Chip/Port (optional)

🔌 OcuLink Expansion: The OcuLink chip/port supports high-speed external storage expansion if needed in the future. Only install the OcuLink chip if you do not plan to use both M.2 slots for SSDs.

If you want to use OcuLink for external storage, follow these steps:

  1. Locate the OcuLink chip/port that comes with the Minisforum AI X1-255 package.
  2. Verify that the chip and connector are clean and free of dust or debris.
  3. Identify the available M.2 2280 slot on the motherboard (typically near the back I/O or expansion area).
  4. Unscrew the screw at the end of the available M.2 slot and set it aside.
  5. Carefully install the OcuLink chip/port into the slot, ensuring proper alignment.
  1. Gently press down until the chip is firmly seated, then use the screw to secure the OcuLink chip/port in place.

Step 5: Close the Case

  1. Carefully replace the back panel, ensuring it aligns properly with the case.
  2. Reinsert and tighten the four screws securely, but do not over-tighten.
  3. Verify the case is properly sealed before proceeding to network setup.
